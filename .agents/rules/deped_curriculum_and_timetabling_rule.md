# DepEd Curriculum & Timetabling Rules

## 1. Curriculum Standards (DepEd K to 12 & MATATAG)
- **Junior High School (Grades 7–10)**:
  - All JHS subjects are Full Year subjects with sequential annual prerequisites.
  - Learning areas: English, Mathematics, Science, Filipino, Araling Panlipunan, EsP, TLE, MAPEH.
- **Senior High School (Grades 11–12)**:
  - Subjects are classified into **Core** (15 subjects), **Applied** (7 subjects), and **Specialized** tracks.
  - Active tracks & strands: STEM, ABM, HUMSS, GAS, TVL-ICT, TVL-HE.
  - Prerequisite linkages must be strictly maintained (e.g., Pre-Calculus ➔ Basic Calculus; FABM 1 ➔ FABM 2 ➔ Business Finance; CSS Mod 1 ➔ Mod 2 ➔ Mod 3 ➔ Mod 4; Bread & Pastry ➔ Cookery 1 ➔ Cookery 2 ➔ FBS).

## 2. Timetable & Schedule Rules
- **No Clustering / Balanced Mon-Fri Distribution**:
  - Timetables must be evenly distributed from Monday to Friday (`07:30 AM` to `02:50 PM`).
  - No empty days and no back-to-back duplicate periods for the same subject on the same day.
- **Zero-Conflict Constraints**:
  1. **Teacher Overlap Constraint**: A teacher can only teach 1 section at any given day and time slot.
  2. **Section Overlap Constraint**: A section cannot have overlapping subjects.
  3. **Room Overlap Constraint**: A room or lab can only host 1 section at any given time slot.
- **Dual-Semester Coverage**:
  - JHS must be marked as `Full Year` so schedules appear in both 1st and 2nd Semester views.
  - SHS sections must have full 5-to-6 subject daily schedules in **both 1st Semester and 2nd Semester**.
